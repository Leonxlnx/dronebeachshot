# Inland outcrop cleanup

Some inland procedural outcrops were stretched into conspicuous tall sheets.
Twenty-one of the worst sheets now use the existing original rock scans,
uniformly scaled and embedded in the actual terrain. The existing 48 near
replacements and 37 tidal rocks retain their geometry and transforms.

The fitting pass preserves the reviewed footprint, caps exposed vertices near
12 metres and keeps at least 60 percent of each scan's vertices below the
terrain. It adds 173,588 geometric triangles and reuses the existing materials
and texture images. The visible scene and coastal worker call the same assembly.
The complete coastal field remains byte-identical to the preceding main.

The first 24-fit study had three upper-wood contact cases. Those fits are
excluded after selection, with no replacement slots refilled. The retained
cohort was checked against the actual opaque tree triangles at all three LODs,
including growth forms, closed-scan interior classification and a conservative
bound on wind displacement. This does not certify leaf-card clearance or fix
all contacts in the pre-existing procedural geology.

Three paired scene views support a bounded improvement: the most distracting
tall sheets are reduced. The actual integrated close view is byte-identical to
the reviewed candidate. Broad pale slopes, sparse canopy and rock/terrain
material continuity still require art work. Native diagnostic frames do not
replace browser validation.

Validation: 54 tests, asset and source-form checks, full world construction,
TypeScript and the production build pass. Regression checks bind the reviewed
source geometry, cohort transforms and tree placements, and verify embedding,
uniform scale, root corridors, near/tidal preservation and the full coastal
field. Changes to these inputs require another geometry and image review.

The original final art, browser, gallery, film and active-time requirements
remain open. Separate Jacaranda and terrain studies are not included as
production assets or terrain changes in this checkpoint.
